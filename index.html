FAQ

Questions? We got you.

How much does junk removal cost?

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck, plus any heavy or specialty items. Use our online estimate tool for a ballpark price, or send photos for an exact quote.

Do I need to be home during the pickup?

Not always. If items are outside or accessible, we can do a contactless pickup — just let us know where everything is and pay online.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, when our schedule allows. Call or text early in the day for the best chance at a same-day pickup.

What happens to my stuff?

We sort every load. Usable items get donated, metals and electronics get recycled, and the rest is disposed of properly at licensed facilities.

What are your hours?

We work every day, generally 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Book online through Booksy or contact us to schedule.

Do you serve Lake Tahoe and Carson City?

Absolutely. We regularly serve Carson City, Incline Village, South Lake Tahoe and surrounding areas. A small travel fee may apply outside Reno/Sparks.
